Why The Alpha Team

Built by Creators, for SRMCEM

We bridge the gap between creative campus passion and viral digital storytelling. Documenting iconic college moments, fests, and student talent for almost 4 years.

4 Years of Storytelling & Digital Media at SRMCEM

The Alpha Team was founded on the campus of Shri Ramswaroop Memorial College of Engineering and Management (SRMCEM, Lucknow) on a simple mission: bringing college life, student talent, and iconic campus festivals into the digital spotlight through cinematic reels, event coverage, and creative storytelling.

Led by Aditya Rawat, our crew of 15+ dedicated student creators, videographers, editors, and interviewers has successfully conducted and covered 10+ major campus events—spanning annual cultural fests like Abhivyakti, technical battles in Gantavya, sports meets, freshers' carnivals, and viral vox pop interviews.

Our Capabilities

What We Do

From full-scale event coverage and viral reels to candid student interviews and stage hype—here is how The Alpha Team powers SRMCEM's digital presence.

Event Coverage

Capture and document college events—including annual cultural fest Abhivyakti, Gantavya tech fests, sports tournaments, and student celebrations.

Content Creation

Create engaging reels, trending audio hooks, campus comedy skits, and relatable social media content tailored to college students.

Editing & Production

Edit raw campus footage into snappy reels, dynamic motion graphics, sound-designed teasers, and cinematic fest highlight aftermovies.

Event Promotion

Create teaser videos, countdown reels, aesthetic posters, and promotional social campaigns that maximize student turnout for college fests.

Event Organisation

Organise and execute creative campus events, video competitions, open mic corners, and collaborative student showcases from end to end.

Creative Setups

Design aesthetic campus photo booths, custom neon selfie backdrops, 360-degree video rigs, and interactive festival installations.

Faculty & Student Interviews

Conduct candid campus vox pops, trending opinion polls, professor interviews, and student achievement spotlights that connect the campus.

Social Media Growth

Help club members understand algorithms, master camera presence, refine portfolio reels, and build a strong personal creator brand.
